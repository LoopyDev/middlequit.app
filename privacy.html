Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

MiddleQuit is made by Mat Trocha, trading as LoopyDev, in the United Kingdom. This page explains what information the app, this website and the checkout handle. If you have a question or want your information removed, email support@middlequit.app.

The app

MiddleQuit doesn't collect, store or send any personal information. It has no network code, so it doesn't connect to the internet, and it has no accounts, analytics or tracking. You can check this yourself in the source code.

  • Accessibility permission: MiddleQuit uses this to see clicks on the Dock and work out which app is under your cursor, so it can quit it. It doesn't record or keep anything it sees.
  • Trackpad: to recognise a three-finger tap, MiddleQuit reads where your fingers touch the trackpad as it happens. These touches are used straight away and never stored.
  • Settings: your choices, such as which click quits an app, are saved on your Mac in its normal app preferences. They never leave your Mac.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics or tracking, and it loads everything, including fonts, from its own address. It's hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may log technical details such as your IP address to run and protect its service. See GitHub's privacy statement. The AlternativeTo badge links to AlternativeTo, which has its own privacy policy if you follow the link.

Buying MiddleQuit

Purchases are handled by Polar, which acts as the merchant of record. That means Polar is the seller, takes your payment, handles tax and sends your receipt. Polar collects the details needed for that, such as your name, email address, country and payment details, under Polar's privacy policy. Card details are handled by Polar's payment providers and are never shared with me.

Polar shares your name, email address and order details with me so I can help with your order, send updates about MiddleQuit if you ask for them, and handle refunds. I don't sell or share this information with anyone else.

Emailing me

Emails to support@middlequit.app are forwarded by Cloudflare to my inbox, which is hosted by my web host, Namecheap. I keep emails only as long as I need them to help you, and I'll delete them on request.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you can ask to see the information I hold about you, have it corrected or have it deleted. Email support@middlequit.app and I'll reply within 30 days. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
